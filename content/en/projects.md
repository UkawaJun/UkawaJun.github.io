Kaempferol, luteolin and quercetin from star anise, mapped onto multi-target gastric-cancer biology.

Star anise is both spice and traditional medicine. Flavonoids such as quercetin, luteolin and kaempferol show anti-gastric-cancer potential, but the material basis is still unclear.

Key flavonoids from TCMSP, 243 intersecting gastric-cancer targets, a STRING/Cytoscape core of seven nodes, and docking of AKT1 and TP53. PyMOL for the kaempferol–AKT1 pose.

- TCMSP screen of kaempferol, luteolin, quercetin; 243 intersecting targets.
- Seven core nodes; docked AKT1 and TP53; PyMOL view of kaempferol on AKT1.
